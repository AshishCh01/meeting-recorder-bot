Meeting Recorder Bot Architecture and Lifecycle Overview

Google Meet·18 Sept, 13:16·2 min
TL;DR

The team walked through the recorder bot end to end: how a submitted link is validated against the platform allow list, how Chrome is launched with a saved session, and where the recording goes once the call ends. Billing was deferred to October so calendar sync can ship first.

Key points

Submitted URLs are checked against an allow list before a bot is ever launched, so unsupported platforms fail fast.

The recorder runs headed Chrome on a virtual display and authenticates from a saved session state rather than logging in each time.

Audio is captured with ffmpeg and uploaded when the call ends; progress pings keep the dashboard current.

Calendar sync ships before billing. Priya owns the pricing copy, Dan sizes the migration.
