FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Does MeetIQ record video?

No. The bot captures audio only. No video is recorded or stored at any point.

Do my guests get an email?

Never. MeetIQ sends nothing to anyone in the call. The notes go to you and stay with you.

Do I need to tell people they're being recorded?

Yes. The bot joins as a visible participant under a name you choose, so everyone can see it is there. Recording laws vary by country, so tell participants and get their agreement before you start.

Which platforms are supported?

Google Meet and Zoom today, from a pasted link or a synced Google Calendar event.

Can I delete a recording?

Yes. Deleting a meeting removes the audio, the transcript and the chat history together.

Will the bot join meetings on its own?

Only for calendar events you switch on one by one. Connecting your calendar shows your upcoming meetings; it does not record any of them.
